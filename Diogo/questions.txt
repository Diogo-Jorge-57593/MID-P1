### Questions
1. Select a relevant application domain and define the problem to be addressed:
    * The application domain is banking, I will focus on customes records and accounts, transactions and credit applications
    * Banking information is often spread across multiple heterogenous sources, customer profiles, accounts and applications for credit will be stored as documents inside MongoDB, and movements/transactions will be stored in csv files, extracted from another source.
    * The separation makes it hard to obtain one single view of a customers identity and activity
    * We will model and populate these sources, prepare the integration layer between them and relate them

2. Identify the stakeholders and formulate user requirements with input from domain experts or relevant stakeholders whenever possible
    * The stakeholders may be inthis case: the bank customer, who should seek to understand and review it´s own activity
    * Credit officer, who will need to review information in order to accept/deny a credit 
    * Bank manager, who will need to monitor banking activity, credit´s accepted/denied or pending, who may need to review an employees performance, allowed or denied credits or time taken in each vredit approval


### Deconstruction: 
* What information is most useful when answering customer enquires? 
    * Account and transaction details: amount, date, description and processing status. [1]
    * Related dispute details and claim status, where applicable. [1]
* Which tasks require information from multiple systems?
    * Investigating disputed transactions: account, transaction and claim information. [1]
    * Reviewing credit applications: applicant information, income evidence, expenses and credit liabilities. [2]
    * Project requirement: link MongoDB accounts to CSV transactions through account IDs (inferred).
* What information is most useful when reviewing a credit application?
    * Age, employment situation, regular income and expenses. [2]
    * Existing credit commitments and potential changes affecting repayment capacity. [2]
    * Documents confirming information is accurate and current. [2]

Note: Answers summarise published guidance; no direct stakeholder interviews were conducted.
